02Messaging and tone

Professional. Collaborative. Approachable.

We sound capable, engaged and supportive. We respect our audience's expertise, use formal Sie in German and make complex information easy to act on.

Copy formulas

Use these formulas to build a clear Professionals message. The examples demonstrate structure. Product claims, figures and feature wording still need an approved source.

Formula
Professionals examples

Headline

One business benefit in six words or fewer„Mehr {Nutzen} für Sie”„{Ziel} schneller erreichen”„Mehr {Nutzen}”For an umbrella message, use the approved Mehr triad
  • „Mehr Sichtbarkeit für Ihre Objekte.”
  • „Schneller passende Kontakte gewinnen.”
  • „Leerstände effizienter reduzieren.”
  • „Mehr Möglichkeiten. Mehr Erfolg. Mehr für Sie.”

Subline

One sentence that supports the headline with a concrete reason. Do not introduce a second benefit.

  • „Präsentieren Sie Ihre Objekte dort, wo Interessierte bereits suchen.”
  • „Gewinnen Sie qualifizierte Kontakte für Ihre Vermarktung.”
  • „Machen Sie Ihre Projekte frühzeitig für passende Käufer sichtbar.”

Call to action

A formal verb phrase with two to four words. Use sentence case and no punctuation at the end.

  • „Beratung anfragen”
  • „Lösungen entdecken”
  • „Kontakt aufnehmen”
  • „Mehr erfahren”

Proof and feature discipline

Use approved wording only

Reuse verified product facts exactly as supplied by the product owner or live approved source. Never invent a feature, number, saving, package inclusion or performance claim.

  • Connect every proof point to one selected audience need
  • Keep tier and package limitations next to the claim
  • Copy mandatory legal and offer lines verbatim

To confirm

The approved Professionals feature vocabulary, proof points, package cautions and fixed legal or offer lines have not yet been supplied. Until they are approved, write about the audience outcome and do not present it as a product fact.

How the voice behaves

Professional
Use precise terms, active verbs and credible evidence.
Avoid inflated promises, vague superlatives and unexplained jargon.
Collaborative
Write as a capable partner who understands the reader's work.
Avoid commands, pressure and language that talks down to experts.
Approachable
Use everyday language and one idea per sentence.
Avoid corporate theatre, filler words and complex metaphors.
Engaged and supportive
Make the next step feel relevant, clear and achievable.
Avoid urgency without evidence and unnecessary exclamation marks.

Do and do not

Do: one clear benefit
„Mehr Sichtbarkeit für Ihre Objekte.”

Short, specific and written for a professional need.

Do not: urgency theatre
„Sichern Sie sich sofort unglaubliche Premiumvorteile!!!”

Stacked punctuation, vague value and pressure without evidence.

Do: concrete proof
„Präsentieren Sie Ihre Objekte dort, wo Interessierte bereits suchen.”

One relevant reason supports the headline without adding another promise.

Do not: vague superlatives
„Unsere innovative Komplettlösung maximiert Ihren Erfolg.”

No specific benefit, no source and no credible proof.

Do: formal and direct action
„Beratung anfragen”

A clear formal verb phrase with no punctuation at the end.

Do not: empty action
„Jetzt zugreifen!!!”

Pressure replaces useful information and a meaningful next step.

06Typography

Make It Better does all the work

Use Make It Better everywhere. The main page heading is the only Extra Bold role. Every other heading uses Bold. Reading copy uses Regular.

Clear value for professionals

Calm body copy gives the explanation enough room to be understood. Use sentence case, short paragraphs and one idea per sentence.

08Imagery

Professional yet approachable

Show competent people in believable working moments. Use natural interaction, warm soft light, relevant German environments and enough negative space for the message.

The image should feel

  • Competent and authentic
  • Warm, soft and naturally lit
  • Professional without looking staged
  • Calm enough to support copy and logo
  • Relevant to the selected audience and need

The Professionals world

Treatments

Composition

Work at eye level by default. Mix wider context with close portraits. Protect faces and meaningful action.

Light and color

Keep light slightly warm, soft and diffused. Use subtle cohesive grading and natural skin tones.

Space

Preserve clean side space for approved copy, logo and one graphic element. Never place copy over a face.

Do and do not

Do: show a believable professional momentUse natural interaction, relevant tools and protected space.
Do not: add heavy effectsAvoid oversaturation, extreme contrast and artificial treatment.
Do not: cover a face or actionMove copy into the available negative space.
Source missing
Do not: use an unapproved sourceConfirm the exact file, licence and crop before export.
Do not: create corporate theatreAvoid forced posing, empty gestures and generic stock behaviour.
Do: protect calm space for the messageKeep copy, logo and one graphic element away from faces.
09Layout templates

One message. One clear path.

Every layout starts with a clear field, one headline, one supporting thought, one action and one approved logo. Add one image or one highlighter only when it strengthens the message.

Build an asset in four decisions

The Studio handles brand values and responsive fit. You provide four clear decisions and complete the human review.

1Choose the audienceSelect one segment, one decision maker and one real need.
2Choose the messageUse one approved benefit and one verified proof point within the copy limits.
3Choose the layoutPick one editable family and an approved image. A new choice must change the composition, not only the colour.
4Choose the channelLet the Studio adapt crop and hierarchy, then review source, claim, logo and fit.
